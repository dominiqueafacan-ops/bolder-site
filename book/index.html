BOLDER

Life Lessons from People Older and Wiser Than You

Bolder was made into a book, published by Hardie Grant in 2019 and featuring both new and old Bolder interviews.

"A beautiful book which I am very in love with... So life-affirming and desperately needed in a culture that is so woefully phobic of age."

— Dolly Alderton

The book was serialised in the Daily Mail and was later optioned by Universal.
