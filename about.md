![BOLDER founders Dominique Afacan and Helen Cathcart](/bolder-site/assets/bolder-founders.jpg)

**BOLDER** celebrates the stories, experiences and wisdom of remarkable older people. Created by writer Dominique Afacan and photographer Helen Cathcart, the project challenges assumptions about what getting older looks and feels like.

This new home for BOLDER brings together interviews and portraits from the original archive, alongside the book *Bolder: Life Lessons from People Older and Wiser Than You*.
